    How are languages and symbols handled?

    In Auto mode, each letter rotates within the alphabet of its own language: Latin, Arabic (including Persian), Cyrillic, Greek, Hebrew, and letters like é and ü. Japanese, Korean, Chinese, Hindi and Thai rotate within their own character ranges. Digits rotate from 0 to 9. Spaces and symbols stay as they are.

    In Full Unicode mode, every letter, symbol and emoji moves through the whole Unicode table, so spaces and symbols are encrypted too. Rail Fence works on every character, space and symbol as it is.

    Automatic cracking relies on letter frequencies in English, Arabic and Russian. The longer the text, the more accurate the result.
